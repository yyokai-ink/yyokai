FAQ
Booking & appointments ⌄
Is a deposit required? +
Yes. The deposit secures your date and is deducted from the final price.
How long does a session last? +
Session length depends on size, placement and complexity.
Can I reschedule? +
Yes, but please do it in advance. Late cancellations may lose the deposit.
Design & style ⌄
Can I bring references? +
Yes. References help define mood, but the final design is custom.
Do you copy tattoos? +
No. Every project is created individually.
Preparation & aftercare ⌄
How should I prepare? +
Sleep well, eat before the session and avoid alcohol before your appointment.
Do you provide aftercare instructions? +
Yes. Detailed aftercare instructions are provided after the session.
Client agreement & consent ⌄
What do I agree to when booking? +
By booking, you confirm that you understand the process, deposit policy and session terms.
