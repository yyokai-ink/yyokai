How to book
01. Booking a session
⌄

To book a tattoo session, please send a description of your idea, the desired placement, and the approximate size of the tattoo.

If you have reference images or examples of work you like, feel free to attach them. This helps me better understand the direction of the project.

Each tattoo is discussed individually. Once the concept, date, and pricing are agreed upon, the appointment is confirmed after the deposit has been received.

02. Deposit
⌄

A deposit is required to secure your appointment.

The deposit is deducted from the final tattoo price and reserves your selected date.

Without a deposit, the appointment date remains available and may be booked by another client.

03. Rescheduling & cancellation
⌄

If the appointment is cancelled too late, the deposit may be non-refundable.

Appointments may be rescheduled in advance, depending on availability.

04. Booking terms & client agreement
⌄

By booking an appointment, paying a deposit, or attending a tattoo session, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to all terms and policies.
