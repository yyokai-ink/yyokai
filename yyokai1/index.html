About

I’m Valerii, a tattoo artist working under the name YYOKAI. I specialize in dark abstraction, custom blackwork and large-scale projects. My work isn’t about copying references — every piece is designed individually to fit the body and create a unique visual impact.

How to book
01. Booking a session
⌃

To book a tattoo session, send a description of your idea, desired placement and approximate size.

If you have references, attach them. They help me understand the mood and direction.

Book an appointment →
02. Deposit
⌄

A deposit is required to secure your appointment. It is deducted from the final tattoo price.

Without a deposit, the date remains available for other clients.

03. Rescheduling
⌄

Appointments can be rescheduled in advance. Late cancellations may result in losing the deposit.

04. Client agreement
⌄

By booking an appointment, paying a deposit or attending a session, you confirm that you agree with all booking terms.

FAQ
Booking & Appointments →
Is a deposit required? +
Yes. A deposit is required to secure your appointment and is deducted from the final price.
How long does a session last? +
Session length depends on the project size, placement and skin condition.
Can I reschedule? +
Yes, but please do it in advance. Late cancellations may lose the deposit.
Design & Style →
Can I bring references? +
Yes. References help define the mood, but the final design is created custom.
Preparation & Aftercare →
How should I prepare? +
